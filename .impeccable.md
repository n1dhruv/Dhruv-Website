## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, potential collaborators, and clients reviewing Dhruv Sharma's work quickly on desktop or mobile. Their primary job is to understand his role, experience, projects, technical strengths, and ways to contact him without navigating a marketing-style site.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, precise, and credible. The experience should feel calm and self-assured: a carefully typeset CV on the web, with the work and writing carrying the personality.

### Aesthetic Direction
A dark, single-column, text-first portfolio inspired by the restraint of dawgdevv.tech. Use a near-black neutral canvas, off-white text, one muted link accent, thin dividers, and generous whitespace. The page should read continuously from identity and introduction through experience, projects, skills, and contact. Avoid gradients, glass effects, shadows, decorative cards, oversized illustrations, bright multi-color palettes, and attention-seeking motion.

### Design Principles
1. Content before decoration: every element must improve scanning, comprehension, or access to the work.
2. Hierarchy through typography: distinguish information using size, weight, spacing, and placement rather than ornamental color or containers.
3. One readable flow: keep the primary experience within a centered 650–750px column and organize it like a concise resume.
4. Restraint is the signature: use one muted accent only for links and interaction; motion is limited to 150–200ms state transitions.
5. Accessible by default: semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, strong contrast, comfortable line-height, responsive spacing, and reduced-motion support.
